Your body does not know the workday ended. Here is how to tell it, in 10 minutes.

Stress Relief & Rest is one of the nine dimensions on the Wholeness Wheel, and it is about more than sleep. It is how you come down from the day so the next one starts lighter. Four things that help, none of them cost anything:

1. An hour before bed, overhead lights off and one lamp on. Low light is the cue your brain is waiting for.
2. Write tomorrow's top three on paper. Three, not twenty. Then close the notebook.
3. Breathe in for a count of 4 and out for a count of 6. Ten rounds, about two minutes.
4. Your phone charges in the kitchen tonight. A ten dollar alarm clock covers the rest.
