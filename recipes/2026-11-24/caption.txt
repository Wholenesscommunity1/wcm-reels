Pumpkin pie for breakfast, 5 minutes, no oven: Greek yogurt parfait

Protein from the yogurt, vitamin A from the pumpkin, fiber from the fruit.

Pumpkin Pie Greek Yogurt Parfait
Serves 2. Prep 5 min, cook none.

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups plain Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup canned pumpkin puree (not pie filling)
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup low-sugar granola
- 1/4 cup toasted pecans, chopped
- 1 apple or pear, diced (or pomegranate seeds)

Steps:
1. Stir the yogurt with the pumpkin, maple syrup, pumpkin pie spice and vanilla until smooth.
2. Layer in 2 glasses: pumpkin yogurt, granola, diced apple. Repeat.
3. Top with the toasted pecans and a pinch more spice.
4. Make ahead: the pumpkin yogurt keeps 3 days in the fridge. Add the granola just before eating so it stays crunchy.
