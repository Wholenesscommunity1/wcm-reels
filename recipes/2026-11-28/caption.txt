Mango Chili Lime Paletas
Serves 8 pops. Prep 10 min, cook 4 hr in the freezer.

Ingredients:
- 4 cups mango chunks, fresh or frozen (about 2 large ripe mangoes), divided
- 1/2 cup cold water (or coconut water)
- 1/4 cup fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 1 to 2 tbsp honey (skip it if your mangoes are very sweet)
- 1 1/2 tsp chili lime seasoning such as Tajin, divided, plus more for serving
- 8 popsicle molds (3 oz each) or small paper cups with wooden sticks

Steps:
1. Set aside 1/2 cup of the mango and dice it small. Put the other 3 1/2 cups in a blender with the water, lime juice and 1 tablespoon of honey. Blend on high for about 1 minute until completely smooth. Taste and add the second tablespoon of honey only if it needs it.
2. Stir in the diced mango and 1 teaspoon of the chili lime seasoning by hand so the little pieces stay whole.
3. Pour the mix into 8 popsicle molds or 3-ounce paper cups, leaving 1/4 inch of space at the top for the pops to expand. Freeze for 1 hour, push a stick into the center of each, then freeze for at least 4 more hours or overnight.
4. To unmold, run the outside of the molds under warm water for 10 to 20 seconds and pull gently. Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 teaspoon of chili lime seasoning over the pops and add a squeeze of lime. Keep them in the freezer in a zip bag for up to 2 weeks.
