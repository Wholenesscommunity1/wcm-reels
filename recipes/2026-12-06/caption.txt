Sunday prep done: 6 grab-and-go breakfasts, one pan, mostly greens. Persian herb frittata

High in protein from eggs, packed with fresh greens, with healthy fats from walnuts.

Persian Herb Frittata (Kuku Sabzi)
Serves 6. Prep 15 min, cook 35 min.

Ingredients:
- 8 large eggs
- 2 cups finely chopped fresh parsley (about 1 large bunch)
- 2 cups finely chopped fresh cilantro (about 1 large bunch)
- 1 cup finely chopped fresh dill
- 1 cup finely chopped scallions (about 6) or chives
- 1/3 cup walnuts, chopped
- 3 tbsp dried cranberries or barberries, chopped (optional)
- 2 tbsp whole wheat flour (or chickpea flour)
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- Plain Greek yogurt and lemon wedges, to serve

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 350F. Brush a 9-inch oven-safe skillet or pie dish with 1 tablespoon of the olive oil, bottom and sides.
2. Wash the herbs, spin or pat them very dry, and chop them finely along with the scallions (a food processor in short pulses works). Wet herbs make a soggy frittata, so dry them well.
3. Whisk the eggs with the flour, baking powder, turmeric, salt and pepper until smooth. Fold in the herbs, scallions, walnuts and cranberries. It should look like mostly herbs bound by a little egg.
4. Pour the mixture into the pan, smooth the top and drizzle with the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the center is set and a knife comes out clean.
5. Cool 10 minutes, then cut into 6 wedges. Serve warm or cold with a spoonful of yogurt and a squeeze of lemon. Keeps 4 days in the fridge; eat it cold or reheat 45 seconds.
