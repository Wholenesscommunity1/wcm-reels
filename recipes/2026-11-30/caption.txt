Fiber and omega-3s from the chia, protein from the Greek yogurt and vitamin C from the mango.

Make-Ahead Mango Coconut Chia Pudding
Serves 4. Prep 10 min, cook 3 min (plus 4 hours in the fridge).

Ingredients:
- 1 can (13.5 oz) light coconut milk
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt (2 percent)
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup, divided
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 1/2 cup chia seeds
- 2 cups diced ripe mango (fresh, or frozen and thawed)
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1/4 cup unsweetened coconut flakes

Steps:
1. Whisk the coconut milk, Greek yogurt, 1 tablespoon of the honey, the vanilla and the salt in a bowl until smooth. Stir in the chia seeds.
2. Let it sit for 10 minutes, then whisk again to break up any clumps. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight, until thick and spoonable.
3. Toss the mango with the lime juice and the remaining 1 tablespoon of honey. Mash about half of it with a fork so you get a saucy layer with chunks.
4. Toast the coconut flakes in a dry skillet over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring, until golden. Cool on a plate.
5. Divide the pudding among 4 jars, spoon the mango on top and finish with the toasted coconut. Lid on; they keep in the fridge for up to 5 days.
