Dessert in 15 minutes that tastes like vacation: grilled pineapple with lime yogurt

Vitamin C and fiber from the fruit, protein from the yogurt.

Grilled Pineapple with Lime Yogurt
Serves 4. Prep 10 min, cook 8 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 ripe pineapple, peeled, cored, cut into 8 spears
- 1 tsp coconut oil, melted
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt (or coconut yogurt)
- 1 tbsp honey
- Zest and juice of 1 lime
- 3 tbsp unsweetened shredded coconut
- A few fresh mint leaves

Steps:
1. Toast the coconut in a dry pan over medium for 2 to 3 minutes until golden, then set it aside.
2. Brush the pineapple spears with melted coconut oil and dust them with cinnamon.
3. Grill on a hot grill pan over medium-high for 3 to 4 minutes per side until caramelized with dark marks.
4. Stir the yogurt with the honey, lime zest and half the lime juice.
5. Plate the pineapple, spoon the lime yogurt over, squeeze on the rest of the lime and top with coconut and mint.
