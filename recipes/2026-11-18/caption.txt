Tandoori chicken without a tandoor: one sheet pan, 25 minutes in the oven

Lean protein with yogurt and warming spices instead of heavy sauce.

Sheet Pan Tandoori Chicken with Raita
Serves 4. Prep 15 min, plus 30 min to marinate, cook 25 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- Marinade: 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Raita: 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 tbsp chopped mint
- Pinch of salt and ground cumin
- Lemon wedges to serve

Steps:
1. Mix the marinade, coat the chicken well and refrigerate for 30 minutes or up to overnight.
2. Heat the oven to 425F. Toss the onion and pepper with the oil on a lined sheet pan and lay the chicken on top.
3. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165F inside. Broil for 2 minutes for charred edges.
4. Stir the raita: yogurt, cucumber, mint, salt and cumin.
5. Serve the chicken and vegetables with raita, lemon wedges and brown rice or warm naan.
