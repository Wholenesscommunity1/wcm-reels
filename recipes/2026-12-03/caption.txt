Holiday dessert with no cream and no refined sugar: chocolate avocado mousse, 10 minutes, and nobody guesses the secret

Fiber and healthy fats from the avocado, antioxidants from the cocoa, no refined sugar.

Mexican Chocolate Avocado Mousse
Serves 4. Prep 10 min, cook none, 30 min to chill.

Ingredients:
- 2 large ripe avocados (about 1 lb)
- 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/4 cup pure maple syrup, plus more to taste
- 1/4 cup unsweetened almond milk (or any milk)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/8 tsp cayenne or ancho chili powder (optional)
- Pinch of salt
- 1 cup fresh raspberries
- 2 tbsp sliced almonds, toasted
- 1 tbsp dark chocolate shavings (optional)

Steps:
1. Halve and pit the avocados and scoop the flesh into a food processor or a strong blender.
2. Add the cocoa powder, maple syrup, almond milk, vanilla, cinnamon, cayenne if using and the salt. Blend 1 to 2 minutes, scraping down the sides, until completely smooth and glossy with no green streaks.
3. Taste. Add another tablespoon of maple syrup for a sweeter mousse, or a splash more milk if it is too thick to spoon.
4. Divide among 4 small glasses or ramekins, cover and chill for at least 30 minutes. It keeps covered in the fridge for up to 3 days.
5. Just before serving, top each glass with raspberries, toasted almonds and a few chocolate shavings.
