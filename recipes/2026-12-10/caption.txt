4 lunches, 35 minutes: Vietnamese lemongrass chicken noodle bowls that beat takeout

Lean protein with a pile of crunchy vegetables and fresh herbs, and very little oil.

Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken Noodle Bowls (Bun Ga Nuong)
Serves 4. Prep 20 min (plus 15 min to marinate), cook 15 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 1/4 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 stalks lemongrass, tender inner core finely minced (about 3 tbsp), or 2 tbsp lemongrass paste
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (for the marinade)
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar or honey
- 1 tbsp avocado or neutral oil
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 8 oz rice vermicelli noodles
- Dressing (nuoc cham): 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 3 tbsp fresh lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 3 tbsp warm water
- 1 tbsp sugar or honey
- 1 small clove garlic, minced
- 1 red chili, thinly sliced (optional)
- 4 cups shredded romaine or butter lettuce
- 1 English cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- 2 cups shredded carrots
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- 1 cup fresh mint and cilantro leaves (Thai basil too, if you can find it)
- 1/4 cup roasted peanuts, chopped
- Lime wedges, to serve

Steps:
1. Stir the lemongrass, garlic, 2 tbsp fish sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar, oil and pepper. Coat the chicken and marinate 15 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
2. Boil the vermicelli 3 to 4 minutes (or soak 5 minutes in just-boiled water), drain, rinse cold and drain well.
3. Whisk 3 tbsp fish sauce, the lime juice, warm water, sugar, garlic and chili until the sugar dissolves.
4. Cook the chicken in a hot skillet or grill pan over medium-high, 5 to 6 minutes per side, until charred and 165F inside. Rest 5 minutes and slice.
5. Layer 4 bowls: lettuce, noodles, cucumber, carrots and sprouts, then chicken, herbs and peanuts.
6. Add 3 tbsp dressing and a squeeze of lime right before eating. For meal prep, pack the herbs and dressing on the side; keeps 4 days in the fridge.
