The reset dinner after a long holiday weekend: Chinese tomato eggs, one pan, 15 minutes

High in protein, with vitamin C from the tomatoes and whole grain fiber from the brown rice.

15-Minute Chinese Tomato Egg Stir-Fry
Serves 2. Prep 5 min, cook 10 min.

Ingredients:
- 4 large eggs
- 1 tbsp water
- 1/2 tsp salt, divided
- 4 ripe medium tomatoes (about 1 1/2 lb), cut into 8 wedges each
- 2 scallions, white and green parts sliced and kept separate
- 1 tbsp avocado or other neutral oil, divided
- 1 tsp sugar (or honey)
- 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1/4 tsp white pepper (or black pepper)
- 2 cups cooked brown rice, warm, for serving

Steps:
1. Beat the eggs with the water, 1/4 teaspoon of the salt and the white parts of the scallions until smooth. The splash of water keeps the eggs soft and fluffy.
2. Heat 2 teaspoons of the oil in a large nonstick skillet or wok over medium-high until it shimmers. Pour in the eggs, let them set for 20 seconds, then push them gently into big soft curds for about 1 minute. Scoop them out while still a little glossy; they finish cooking later.
3. Add the last teaspoon of oil and the tomato wedges to the same pan. Cook over medium-high, stirring now and then, for 4 to 5 minutes until the tomatoes soften and release their juice. Press a few wedges with the spatula so they break down into a sauce.
4. Stir in the sugar, soy sauce, the remaining 1/4 teaspoon of salt and the white pepper. Simmer for 1 minute until the sauce is glossy and lightly thickened.
5. Return the eggs to the pan and fold them gently into the tomatoes for about 1 minute, just until coated and set. Turn off the heat, scatter the scallion greens on top and serve right away over 1 cup of warm brown rice per person.
