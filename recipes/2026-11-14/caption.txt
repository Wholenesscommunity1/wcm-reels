Weekend breakfast in 15 minutes that holds you until lunch: huevos rancheros

Protein from eggs and beans plus fiber from corn tortillas and avocado.

15-Minute Huevos Rancheros
Serves 2. Prep 5 min, cook 10 min.

Ingredients:
- 4 corn tortillas (6 inch)
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup canned black beans, rinsed
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 cup salsa (jarred or fresh)
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 tbsp crumbled queso fresco or feta
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- 1 lime, cut in wedges
- 1 tbsp olive oil, divided

Steps:
1. Warm the beans with the cumin and 2 tablespoons of water in a small pan over medium for 3 minutes, then mash them lightly.
2. Warm the salsa in a small pot or in the microwave for 1 minute.
3. Heat 1 teaspoon of oil in a skillet over medium-high and toast the tortillas 30 seconds per side until lightly crisp. Set aside.
4. Add the rest of the oil and fry the eggs over medium for about 3 minutes. Cover for the last minute so the whites set and the yolks stay soft.
5. Stack 2 tortillas on each plate, spread on the beans, top with 2 eggs, warm salsa, avocado, cheese, cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
