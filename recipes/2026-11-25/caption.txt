Homemade cranberry sauce in 15 minutes with no refined sugar: make it tonight, it tastes better tomorrow

Fiber and vitamin C from whole cranberries, with far less added sugar than canned.

Maple Orange Cranberry Sauce
Serves 10 (about 2 1/2 cups). Prep 5 min, cook 15 min.

Ingredients:
- 12 oz fresh or frozen cranberries (one bag, about 3 cups)
- 1/3 cup pure maple syrup
- 1 tbsp orange zest (from 1 orange)
- 1/2 cup fresh orange juice (1 to 2 oranges)
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 cinnamon stick (or 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon)
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (optional)
- Pinch of salt

Steps:
1. Rinse the cranberries and pick out any soft ones. Zest the orange before you juice it.
2. Put the cranberries, maple syrup, orange zest, orange juice, water, cinnamon stick, ginger and salt in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
3. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer and cook 10 to 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until most of the berries have popped and the sauce thickens.
4. Mash a few berries against the side of the pan for a thicker sauce, or leave it chunky. Remove the cinnamon stick.
5. Cool 20 minutes. It thickens more as it cools. Spoon into a jar.
6. Make ahead: it keeps 10 days in the fridge and is even better after a day. Serve cold or at room temperature.
