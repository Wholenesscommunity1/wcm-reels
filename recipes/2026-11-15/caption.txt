One pot, feeds 6, freezes beautifully: West African peanut stew

Plant protein and fiber from chickpeas, vitamin A from sweet potato.

West African Peanut Stew
Serves 6. Prep 15 min, cook 35 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional)
- 2 medium sweet potatoes (about 1 1/2 lb), cubed
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained
- 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup natural peanut butter
- 4 cups chopped kale or collard greens
- Salt to taste
- Lime wedges, chopped peanuts and cilantro to serve

Steps:
1. Cook the onion in the oil over medium for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, tomato paste, cumin, paprika and cayenne and stir for 1 minute.
2. Add the sweet potato, tomatoes, chickpeas and broth. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Whisk the peanut butter with 1 cup of hot broth from the pot until smooth, then stir it back in.
4. Add the kale and simmer 10 more minutes, until the sweet potato is fork tender.
5. Salt to taste and finish with lime, chopped peanuts and cilantro. Serve over brown rice.
