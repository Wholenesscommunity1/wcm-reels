Fresh Shrimp Spring Rolls with Peanut Sauce
Serves 4 (8 rolls). Prep 20 min, cook 5 min.

Ingredients:
- 8 rice paper wrappers (8.5 inch)
- 2 oz dried rice vermicelli
- 16 cooked large shrimp, peeled, halved lengthwise
- 1 cup shredded carrot
- 1 cucumber, cut into thin sticks
- 8 butter lettuce leaves
- 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
- Sauce: 3 tbsp natural peanut butter
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 3 to 4 tbsp warm water

Steps:
1. Cover the vermicelli with boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and rinse under cold water.
2. Whisk peanut butter, hoisin, lime juice and soy sauce, then thin with warm water until pourable.
3. Dip one wrapper in warm water for 10 to 15 seconds until just soft and lay it on a damp board.
4. On the bottom third, layer lettuce, a small bundle of noodles, carrot, cucumber and herbs. Set 4 shrimp halves pink side down just above.
5. Fold the bottom over the filling, fold in the sides and roll it up tight. Repeat for 8 rolls.
6. Serve with the peanut sauce. Rolls keep 1 day wrapped in a damp towel in the fridge.
