The golden smoothie: 5 minutes, 2 glasses, mango, ginger and turmeric

Vitamin C, protein and fiber in one glass.

Golden Mango Turmeric Smoothie
Serves 2. Prep 5 min, cook none.

Ingredients:
- 2 cups frozen mango chunks
- 1 ripe banana
- 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 3/4 cup unsweetened almond milk (or any milk)
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- Pinch of black pepper
- Juice of 1/2 lime
- 1 tsp honey (optional)

Steps:
1. Pour the milk into the blender first, then add the yogurt, mango, banana, ginger, turmeric, black pepper and chia seeds.
2. Blend on high for 45 to 60 seconds until completely smooth. Add a splash more milk if it is too thick.
3. Taste and add the lime juice and honey if you want it brighter or sweeter. Blend 5 more seconds.
4. Pour into 2 glasses and top with a few chia seeds. Drink right away or keep in a sealed jar for the day.
