Skip the Friday takeout: jerk chicken bowls, one sheet pan, and Monday lunch is done too

Lean protein, fiber from beans and rice, vitamin C from mango.

Sheet Pan Jerk Chicken Bowls with Mango Salsa
Serves 4. Prep 15 min, cook 35 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- Jerk marinade: 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 tsp ground allspice
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp cayenne (or 1 habanero, minced, for real heat)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 large sweet potato (about 1 lb), cut into 1/2 inch cubes
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 tbsp olive oil for the vegetables
- Mango salsa: 1 ripe mango, diced
- 1/2 cup diced cucumber
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 2 tbsp finely diced red onion
- Juice of 1 lime and a pinch of salt
- 3 cups cooked brown rice (1 cup dry)
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed and warmed
- Lime wedges to serve

Steps:
1. Whisk the marinade: 2 tbsp olive oil, lime juice, soy sauce, honey, allspice, thyme, paprika, cinnamon, nutmeg, cayenne, garlic, salt and pepper. Coat the chicken and rest 15 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.
2. Heat the oven to 425F. Toss the sweet potato, bell pepper and red onion with 1 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt on a lined sheet pan. Roast 10 minutes.
3. Push the vegetables to the edges and lay the chicken in the middle. Roast 20 to 22 minutes, until 165F inside. Broil 2 minutes, rest 5 minutes, then slice.
4. While it roasts, stir the salsa: mango, cucumber, cilantro, red onion, lime juice and a pinch of salt.
5. Build 4 bowls: brown rice, black beans, roasted vegetables, sliced chicken and a big spoon of mango salsa. Serve with lime wedges. Extra bowls keep 4 days in the fridge; pack the salsa separately.
