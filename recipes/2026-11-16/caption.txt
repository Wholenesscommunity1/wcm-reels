Meal prep Monday: cold soba salad, 20 minutes, 4 lunches that get better by Wednesday

Plant protein from edamame and buckwheat with plenty of fiber.

Cold Soba Noodle Salad with Edamame
Serves 4. Prep 15 min, cook 5 min.

Ingredients:
- 8 oz soba (buckwheat) noodles
- 1 1/2 cups frozen shelled edamame
- 1 English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 cup shredded red cabbage
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- Dressing: 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 clove garlic, grated

Steps:
1. Boil the soba for 4 to 5 minutes as the package says, adding the edamame for the last 2 minutes. Drain and rinse under cold water until cool.
2. Whisk the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, honey, ginger and garlic into a dressing.
3. Toss the noodles and edamame with the cucumber, cabbage, scallions and dressing.
4. Top with sesame seeds. Pack into 4 containers; it keeps 3 days in the fridge.
