Peanut Butter Stuffed Date Bites
Serves 12 bites. Prep 10 min, cook 20 min in the freezer.

Ingredients:
- 12 Medjool dates
- 1/4 cup natural peanut butter (or almond butter)
- 2 tbsp roasted peanuts, chopped
- 2 oz dark chocolate (70 percent), chopped
- 1/2 tsp coconut oil
- Pinch of flaky salt

Steps:
1. Slit each date down one side, pull out the pit and open it like a book.
2. Spoon 1 teaspoon of peanut butter into each date and press in a few chopped peanuts.
3. Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil in the microwave in 20 second bursts, stirring between each.
4. Dip or drizzle half of each date with chocolate and finish with a tiny pinch of flaky salt.
5. Set on parchment and freeze for 20 minutes. Keep them in the freezer for up to a month.
