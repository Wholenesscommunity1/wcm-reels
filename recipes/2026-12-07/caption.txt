Meal prep Monday: Filipino chicken adobo. One pot, 4 lunches, better by Wednesday

Lean protein with fiber from brown rice and green beans.

Lighter Filipino Chicken Adobo Meal Prep Bowls
Serves 4. Prep 10 min, plus 15 min to marinate, cook 40 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
- 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 1/3 cup white vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)
- 1/2 cup water
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 3 bay leaves
- 1 tsp whole black peppercorns (or 1/2 tsp cracked black pepper)
- 1 tbsp avocado or olive oil
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1 tsp brown sugar or honey (optional)
- 1 cup brown rice (about 3 cups cooked)
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- 2 scallions, sliced
- Lime wedges, to serve

Steps:
1. Toss the chicken with the soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns in a bowl and let it marinate 15 minutes (or overnight). Meanwhile start the rice: 1 cup brown rice, 2 cups water, covered, on low for 40 to 45 minutes.
2. Heat the oil in a large pot or deep skillet over medium-high. Lift the chicken out of the marinade (keep the marinade) and sear 3 minutes per side until browned. Add the onion and cook 2 minutes.
3. Pour in the marinade and the water, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 20 minutes.
4. Uncover and simmer 8 to 10 minutes more, until the sauce reduces to a glaze and the chicken reaches 165F inside. Taste and stir in the sugar or honey if you like it a touch sweeter.
5. Steam the green beans in a skillet with 1/4 cup water, covered, for 4 minutes until bright green and tender-crisp.
6. Divide the rice between 4 containers, top with the chicken and sauce, add the green beans, scallions and a lime wedge. Keeps 4 days in the fridge; reheat 90 seconds. The flavor gets better by day two.
