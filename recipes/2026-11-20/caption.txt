Shrimp and grits, lightened up: 30 minutes, big flavor, no heavy cream

Lean protein from shrimp with whole-grain stone-ground grits.

Lighter Shrimp and Grits
Serves 4. Prep 10 min, cook 25 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 cup stone-ground grits
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 cups water
- 1/2 cup sharp cheddar, shredded
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp cayenne
- 1/4 tsp salt, plus more for the grits
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth (for the sauce)
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 3 scallions, sliced
- Black pepper

Steps:
1. Bring the 2 cups broth and 2 cups water to a boil, whisk in the grits, turn to low, cover and simmer 20 to 25 minutes, stirring now and then. Stir in the cheddar and black pepper.
2. While the grits cook, toss the shrimp with smoked paprika, garlic powder, cayenne and salt.
3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook the bell pepper for 3 minutes, then the garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Add the shrimp and cook about 2 minutes per side, until pink and curled.
5. Pour in the 1/2 cup broth and lemon juice and simmer 1 minute to make a light sauce. Stir in the scallions and spoon over the grits.
