One pan, 30 minutes: lemon chicken orzo that tastes like a Greek island

Lean protein and iron-rich greens in one pan.

One-Pan Lemon Chicken Orzo
Serves 4. Prep 10 min, cook 25 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup dry orzo (whole wheat if you can find it)
- 2 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 4 cups baby spinach
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill or parsley

Steps:
1. Season the chicken with oregano, salt and pepper. Sear in 1 tablespoon oil over medium-high for 5 to 6 minutes until golden and cooked through. Remove to a plate.
2. Add the rest of the oil, cook the onion 3 minutes, the garlic 30 seconds, then toast the orzo for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the broth and lemon zest. Simmer, stirring often, for 10 to 12 minutes until the orzo is tender and creamy.
4. Stir in the spinach, tomatoes, lemon juice and the chicken. Cook 2 minutes until the spinach wilts.
5. Top with feta and dill and serve straight from the pan.
