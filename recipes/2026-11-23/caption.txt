Smoky Baba Ganoush
Serves 6. Prep 10 min, cook 40 min.

Ingredients:
- 2 medium eggplants (about 2 lb)
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more to finish
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- Pinch of smoked paprika
- Pomegranate seeds (optional)
- Cucumber, carrots, bell pepper and whole wheat pita to serve

Steps:
1. Heat the oven to 450F. Prick the eggplants all over, set them on a foil-lined sheet pan and roast 35 to 40 minutes, turning once, until collapsed and charred.
2. Cool 10 minutes, split them open and scoop the flesh into a colander. Let it drain 10 minutes and discard the skins.
3. Chop the flesh fine or mash it with a fork, then stir in the tahini, lemon juice, garlic, cumin, salt and olive oil.
4. Spread in a bowl, drizzle with olive oil and top with parsley, smoked paprika and pomegranate seeds.
5. Serve with crunchy vegetables and warm pita. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.
