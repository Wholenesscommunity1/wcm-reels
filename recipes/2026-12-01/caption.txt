20 minutes tonight and lunch is handled for the week: Moroccan chickpea couscous jars, 4 jars, no reheating

Plant protein and fiber from the chickpeas and whole wheat couscous, plus vitamin A from the carrots.

Moroccan Chickpea Couscous Lunch Jars
Serves 4. Prep 15 min, cook 10 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 cup whole wheat couscous
- 1 1/4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 2 medium carrots, grated (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1/2 cup dried apricots, chopped
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh mint
- 1/4 cup sliced almonds
- Dressing: 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp lemon juice (about 1 large lemon)
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional)
- 1 tsp honey
- 1/2 tsp salt

Steps:
1. Bring the broth to a boil in a small pot. Stir in the couscous, cover, take it off the heat and let it stand for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork and spread it on a plate to cool for 5 minutes.
2. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, cayenne, honey and salt in a small bowl until smooth.
3. Toast the almonds in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring, until golden. Tip them onto a plate so they stop cooking.
4. In a large bowl, toss the chickpeas, carrots, bell pepper, apricots, parsley and mint with the dressing. Fold in the cooled couscous.
5. Divide among 4 jars or containers and top with the almonds. Lid on; they keep 4 days in the fridge and taste great cold or at room temperature.
