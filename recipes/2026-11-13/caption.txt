Bibimbap at home in 30 minutes: 5 vegetables, one egg, zero takeout

A balanced plate with lean protein, whole grains and five vegetables.

30-Minute Bibimbap Bowl
Serves 2. Prep 15 min, cook 15 min.

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups cooked short-grain brown rice
- 8 oz lean ground beef (or crumbled firm tofu)
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil, divided
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp avocado oil, divided
- 4 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 1 cup matchstick carrots
- 3 cups baby spinach
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- 2 eggs
- 1 to 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Salt

Steps:
1. Brown the beef in a hot pan over medium-high for 5 to 6 minutes with the soy sauce, 1 teaspoon sesame oil, the sugar and garlic. Set aside.
2. In the same pan with 1 teaspoon oil, cook the mushrooms 3 minutes, then the carrots 2 minutes, then the spinach until just wilted, about 1 minute. Salt each lightly.
3. Blanch the bean sprouts in boiling water for 30 seconds and drain, or use them raw for crunch.
4. Fry the eggs in the last teaspoon of oil over medium for about 3 minutes, until the whites set and the yolks stay runny.
5. Divide the rice between 2 bowls, arrange the vegetables and beef in sections, top with an egg, gochujang, sesame seeds and the rest of the sesame oil. Mix it all up before eating.
