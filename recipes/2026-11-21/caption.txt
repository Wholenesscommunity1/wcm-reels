Chicken salad with no mayo, 15 minutes, and a lunch that actually keeps you full

High in protein with healthy fats from avocado instead of mayo.

No-Mayo Avocado Chicken Salad
Serves 4. Prep 15 min, cook none.

Ingredients:
- 3 cups shredded cooked chicken (a rotisserie chicken works)
- 1 large ripe avocado
- 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp salt
- Black pepper
- 1/4 cup finely diced red onion
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 jalapeno, minced (optional)
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 8 butter or romaine lettuce leaves (or whole-grain toast)

Steps:
1. Mash the avocado with the yogurt, lime juice, cumin, salt and pepper until creamy.
2. Fold in the chicken, red onion, tomatoes, jalapeno and cilantro.
3. Spoon into lettuce leaves for cups, or pile it onto whole-grain toast.
4. Keeps 2 days in the fridge with plastic wrap pressed onto the surface so it stays green.
